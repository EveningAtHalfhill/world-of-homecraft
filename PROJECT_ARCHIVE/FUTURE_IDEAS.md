# FUTURE IDEAS

## Version Roadmap

- V0.1：职业家宅初版
- V0.2：《酒馆与邻居》

The formal display name is `World of Homecraft / 魔坊世界`.

The internal engineering name remains `wow-home-generator`.

- 同盟种族 support with race-specific room flavor.
- 环境音 presets for taverns, forests, crypts, sanctums, arcane towers, and battle halls.
- NPC语音 generated or scripted as short shareable lines.
- 隐藏roll事件 that occasionally add strange events to the generated plan.
- 酒馆传闻系统 with region-based gossip pools.
- 节日限定家具 for Winter Veil, Hallow's End, Lunar Festival, Brewfest, and Darkmoon Faire.
- 稀有世界事件 that unlock temporary affixes, NPC comments, or furniture.
- AI动态生成家宅 with richer prompt-driven room layouts and class fantasy.

## Possible Hidden Events

- Murloc visitor in the bathtub.

- Ogre interior designer arrives uninvited.

Turn the generator into a compact fantasy home design studio where class, race, zone, NPCs, rumors, and world events combine into a shareable living-world poster.
